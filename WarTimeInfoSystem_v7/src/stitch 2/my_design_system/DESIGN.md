# Design System

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, driven by our brand identity.

## Theme Overview

Our design system is currently configured for a **light** color mode, providing a bright and clean user experience.

## Colors

The foundation of our visual identity is built upon a carefully selected color palette.

-   **Primary Color:** The brand's most distinctive chromatic color, used for key interactive elements and calls to action. We use `#1275e2` for our primary actions and branding.
-   **Secondary Color:** A supporting color for less prominent UI elements, chips, and secondary actions. This provides visual hierarchy and a cohesive feel. We use `#5f78a3` for secondary elements.
-   **Tertiary Color:** An additional accent color for highlights, badges, or decorative elements, adding a touch of distinction. We use `#c55b00` for accents.
-   **Neutral Color:** A foundational base color for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements, ensuring readability and visual balance. We use `#74777f` for our neutral tones.

## Typography

Our typographic choices ensure clarity, hierarchy, and brand consistency across all touchpoints.

-   **Headline Font:** `inter` is used for all headlines, providing a modern and impactful presence.
-   **Body Font:** `inter` is also selected for body text, ensuring readability and a consistent look with headlines.
-   **Label Font:** `inter` is applied to labels, maintaining typographic harmony throughout the interface.

## Shape and Form

The geometry of our UI elements contributes significantly to the overall feel and usability.

-   **Roundedness:** Our UI elements feature a **moderate** level of roundedness (set to `2`). This strikes a balance between sharpness and softness, providing a friendly yet professional aesthetic.

## Spacing

The thoughtful application of whitespace enhances readability, defines relationships between elements, and contributes to a clean interface.

-   **Spacing:** We adhere to a **normal** level of spacing (set to `2`). This provides a balanced layout that is neither too dense nor overly expansive, facilitating comfortable user interaction.
